Where The Savings Often Are

LED is only half the story.

The fitting reduces how much electricity the luminaire consumes. The controls reduce how long you pay for it. Combining both is where Canberra facilities cut energy costs by up to 68%.

🚶

Occupancy

Integrated microwave and PIR sensors automatically extinguish or dim lighting when car parks, basement aisles, and stairwells sit vacant.

Eliminates up to 43% of unnecessary burning hours.
🌓

Dimming

Maintain required safety lux (30% background output) without paying for 100% maximum wattage when no pedestrians or vehicles are present.

Cuts standby power draw by 70% during quiet periods.
⏱️

Timing

Astronomical time clocks and ambient daylight harvesting prevent exterior floodlights, facade uplights, and common lobbies running into daylight hours.

Guarantees zero daytime energy leakage.
